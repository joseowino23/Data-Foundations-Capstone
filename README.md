# Data-Foundations-Capstone
Sales Data Analysis: Summary and Recommendations

Project Summary

This project analyzes sales performance and profitability using Tableau visualizations. The analysis focuses on comparing total sales across branches, identifying the most profitable branch within the Electronics category, and examining monthly profit trends throughout 2024.

The dashboards help identify differences in branch performance, branch profitability, and changes in profit over time. These insights can support better business decisions, improve sales strategies, and increase profitability.

Key Areas of Analysis

1. Branch Sales Performance: Compared total sales across branches to identify the highest- and lowest-performing branches.
2. Electronics Profitability: Compared average profit per order across branches to identify the most profitable segment.
3. Monthly Profit Trend: Examined monthly profit from January to December 2024 to identify trends and potential seasonal patterns.

Recommendations: 3 Actionable Insights

1. Improve Performance in Low-Selling Branches

Identify the branch with the lowest total sales and investigate possible causes, such as low customer traffic, limited product availability, or ineffective marketing. Introduce targeted promotions, improve stock availability, and monitor monthly sales to measure improvement.

2. Focus on the Most Profitable Electronics Customer Segment

Prioritize marketing campaigns and product offers for the branch with the highest average profit per order in Electronics. Develop targeted promotions and personalized offers while monitoring profit margins to ensure that increased sales translate into higher profits.

3. Use Monthly Profit Trends to Improve Planning

Review the monthly profit trend for 2024 to identify periods of declining profit and months with stronger performance. Adjust inventory, marketing campaigns, and operating costs based on these patterns. Investigate the causes of profit declines and apply successful strategies from stronger months.

Conclusion

The analysis provides a foundation for improving branch performance, strengthening customer-focused marketing, and making better business decisions. By implementing these recommendations and monitoring key performance indicators regularly, the business can work toward sustainable sales growth and improved profitability.
